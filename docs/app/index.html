What to change in the first team's schedule each one from your own match numbers (Tactical fit → How it's going)

Train the press more: more Out Of Possession weeks

Why

For a gegenpress side the press is the identity, but your press isn't biting hard enough: tackles won 14th of 20; this season you've run In Possession weeks 0×, Out Of Possession 0× and Balanced 5×, so Pressing Intensity and Pressing Trap come round about 6.0× a week.

Change

Alternate Tactical Focus – In Possession and Tactical Focus – Out Of Possession week to week (or use Balanced in weeks with two matches), so Pressing Intensity, Pressing Trap and Out Of Possession Shape come up every week.

Won't hurt

It swaps which tactical sessions you do, not how many — same three sessions a day, same match rhythm, same load. You keep In Possession every other week, so the attacking work isn't dropped.

Leave these alone
  • Aggression is low across your XI (13.7), but you're already 13th of 20 for fouls — training it team-wide would add fouls and cards. Only give it as an individual focus to players who are also poor at winning the ball back.
  • Shots (2nd of 20): constant pressure on goal — don't take out the sessions behind it.
  • Opponents' final-third passes (1st of 20): keeping teams pinned back — don't take out the sessions behind it.
  • xG against (3rd of 20): opponents barely get a sniff — don't take out the sessions behind it.
  • Goals conceded (1st of 20): hard to score against — don't take out the sessions behind it.

Doing it yourself next time how the advice above is worked out · seven steps and a cheat sheet
  1. Find the problem in the numbers, not the attributes. Tactical fit → How it's going lists what's in the bottom half of the league. That's what training should fix.
  2. Match it to a session with the cheat sheet below. Team problems (shape, pressing, chance creation) are team sessions; player problems (finishing, heading) are individual focus.
  3. Swap, don't stack. Every day already has three sessions. Replace a session of the same category, ideally one behind something you're already top of the league for. Adding load is how players get injured.
  4. Keep the match rhythm. Recovery the day after a match, Match Preparation the day before, anything heavy two days after a match. No extra physical work in two-match weeks.
  5. Check FM's own warning. Each schedule shows its effect on injury risk, fatigue and condition — if a change turns those red, undo it.
  6. Individual focus = the lowest trainable attribute that matters for his role or your style, preferring one behind a weak number of his. One at a time.
  7. Re-check in a month. Refresh this report and see if the number moved.
