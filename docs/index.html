What's inside

The real pages, with demo data. Liverpool is the demo club; ratings are made up.

What it does, and what it won't

It does

  • Reads your newest FM27 save by itself, read-only
  • Rates every player from visible attributes, for your tactic and in general
  • Picks a best XI and a second XI
  • Flags positions that need a better starter or more depth
  • Checks your tactic against the league from match stats
  • Shows training, deals, loans and season stats
  • Rebuilds itself every time you save

It won't

  • Change your save. It only ever reads it
  • Show current ability, potential ability or hidden attributes
  • Tell you who to sign. It points at positions, never players
  • Send your save anywhere. It stays on your Mac
  • Ask for an account or a subscription

The one exception, if you want it: potential stars for players 23 and under, switched off with one setting.

How it works

  1. 1

    Save in FM27

    Play as normal with any club. The app watches for your newest save.

  2. 2

    It rebuilds itself

    About 30 seconds after you save, the report is ready.

  3. 3

    Refresh and read

    Open the app, or refresh it if it's already open.

Your save is waiting.
